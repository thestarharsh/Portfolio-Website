## Why this matters

Addy Osmani has been a strong voice in making performance engineering more concrete, practical, and product-aware. It has been energizing to see people engaging with the book and writing about its ideas, because that is exactly where the real value lies: in turning big concepts into day-to-day engineering decisions.

Web performance is no longer just a frontend optimization problem. It is a product, architecture, and AI-era engineering problem. The more AI helps us ship code faster, the more important it becomes to stay deliberate about what we actually ship, how it loads, and what it costs users and businesses.

If you’re thinking about performance in the age of AI, this series is a practical companion to the book and to the thinking behind it.
